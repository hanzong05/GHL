Rules & Ordinances

Park policies for a safe and enjoyable visit

Park Hours & Safety
Park Hours
Both entrances to the Park are open daily from 5AM – Midnight.
Emergencies
Use the Police Call Boxes located throughout the Park, go to any attraction and request the Park Police, or call (770) 413-5333 from any telephone.
Speed Limit
25 MPH on park roads, and 15 MPH in the Campground, parking lots and near playgrounds. Violators can receive a court citation.
Park Policies
Alcoholic Beverages
Consumption or possession of alcoholic beverages is prohibited on streets, parking lots, the Memorial Lawn or any posted area.
Pets
No pets other than dogs and cats are allowed. All dogs and cats must be kept on a leash no longer than 6 feet. Pets are NOT allowed on attractions, the Memorial Lawn, Songbird Habitat and Trails, at special events, or in posted areas. Pet owners are responsible for the removal of pet waste.
Designated Smoking Areas
Please utilize the Designated Smoking Areas at or near each major attraction. Smoking is prohibited inside all buildings, in queue lines, on attractions, and on the Laser Lawn.
Recreation
Public Picnic Areas
Public use tables are available on a first-come, first-served basis. No more than six picnic tables and the immediate area around those tables can be used by a single group. Private outside catering is never allowed. Sound systems cannot carry sound more than 50 feet from the system.
Walk-Up Trail
When climbing the mountain, please stay on the trail marked with a yellow line. If you walk up and ride down the Skyride, there is no transportation back to the Walk-Up Trail parking lot.
Fishing
Georgia Fishing License mandatory (I.D. required). Visit gooutdoorsgeorgia.com or contact the Campground General Store for more info.
Boating
Boats are allowed during daylight hours only. Boats with motors over 10 HP are not allowed. A PFD is required for every occupant. Violators can receive a court citation.
Bicycles
Permitted during daylight hours and after dark with proper lights. Bikes must be kept on paved roads and ridden single file on weekends. Georgia Law requires helmets for all riders under age 16.
Guest Information
Lost & Found
All Lost & Found items are turned in each night to the Operations Department: (770) 498-5696.
Credit Cards
Stone Mountain Park accepts Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express for admission and at most gift shops and restaurants.
Stroller / Wheelchair Rental
Available on a first-come, first-served basis. Rental facility located just inside the Main Ticket Plaza at the Adventure Center.

Park Information
🌙 Quiet Hours
11 PM to 7 AM. For noise complaints call the SMP Police Non-Emergency Number 770-498-5675.
🗑️ Garbage Pickup
Curbside at your site between 9 AM – 4 PM. There are no dumpsters — trash will be picked up curbside only.
👕 Laundry Facilities
Located at Comfort Stations #3, #7 and #9. Use the Aaxon Pay App. A QR Code is in the Laundry Room.
🔍 Lost & Found
Located at the Welcome Center. Technology items (phones, computers, wallets, credit cards) will be turned over to SMP Public Safety.
📦 Mail Drop & Package Delivery
At the Welcome Center. Please include your Name and Site number when having something delivered.
🏊 Swimming Pool Passes
Available in the Welcome Center. You must present one for yourself or family when asked by a staff member.
🚗 UBER / LYFT
Available for guests. Drivers will be charged a $5.00 fee at the entrance gate — please advise your driver in advance.
⚡ Weather Alert System
If lightning is detected within 10 miles of the park a horn will sound once for 15 seconds. The horn sounds 3 times rapidly when lightning has cleared the area. If severe weather is approaching, the system will sound advising you to seek shelter.
