Toonz Boat Rentals

Lake Blue Ridge

At Toonz Rentals of Blue Ridge, we pride ourselves on being family-friendly, locally owned, and dedicated to exceptional customer service. Our top priority is ensuring you and your loved ones have the absolute best and safest time on the lake, creating cherished memories that last a lifetime.

Toonz is located in the beautiful mountain town of Blue Ridge, GA surrounded by picturesque mountains and crystal clear aquamarine waters. The lake is 3,290 acres and has many campsites and mountain homes. When the Blue Ridge Dam was constructed on the Toccoa River in 1930 by the Toccoa Electric Power Company, the lake was formed. Enjoy swimming, boating, fishing, and picnicking on the lake while renting with Toonz.
Meet the Payne Family
Toonz Rentals of Blue Ridge was a dream developed by Jeremy. Toonz was born from the desire to create a welcoming, fun-filled environment for everyone to enjoy the splendor of Lake Blue Ridge. Jeremy and Lauren, natives of the tri-state area, along with their sons Zeke and Eli, have a deep-rooted love for the water, spending countless hours fishing, boating, and soaking up the sun on the local lakes.
Meet the Stiles Family
Thomas and Ashley Stiles with their two children, Kolby and Kinley, are an integral part of what makes Toonz special. Thomas manages the day to day operations to make Toonz what it is. Their son, Kolby, also works at Toonz during his summer break from college.

Our mission at Toonz is to share our love for the water with you and your family, helping you create cherished memories on Lake Blue Ridge. Whether it's a leisurely cruise on one of our brand new Leisure Kraft pontoons or an exhilarating ride on a kayak or stand-up paddleboard, we're here to ensure your experience is nothing short of fantastic.
Meet the Nazerian Family
Stephen and Kayla Nazerian live in Mineral Bluff with their three children, Maggie, Millie and Max. Stephen enjoys playing golf during his time off and works at United Community Bank in Blue Ridge as a Portfolio Manager, where he has worked for 22 years. Kayla is the Office Manager at her parents' grocery store, IGA in McCaysville. In her free time, Kayla likes going camping and making new memories with her family and friends. Maggie enjoys playing softball, Millie loves ballet, tap & jazz, and Max's favorite thing to do is "feed the moo cows."

Old Toccoa Farm Resort

Where Community Is the Real Amenity

Here at Old Toccoa Farm Resort, residents, members, and visiting guests actively participate in an understated mountain lifestyle, steeped in relentless hospitality and a true sense of place.

Our 420+ acre property, conveniently located near the charm of downtown Blue Ridge, sits between expansive protected forest lands filled with twisted knots of mountain laurel, meadows of native grasses, and over 4,000+ feet of frontage along the Toccoa River. The Cherokee Indians referred to this area as the Enchanted Land, and today our members and guests enjoy a menu of activity-based pursuits, including award-winning golf, trophy fly fishing, racquet sports, community walking trails, and managed access to off-property activities such as hiking and boating, as well as rafting and programs in the arts and local culture.

There is a new way of living in the Blue Ridge Mountains of North Georgia, and you will find it here at Old Toccoa Farm Resort.

Golf

#8 Best New Course in America, 2020 – Golf Digest

Nature's infinite presence defines every hole, and every hazard on the Jack Dredla, Dan Proctor, Dave Axland, aka Bunker Hill-designed course. Here, twisted knots of mountain laurel, rock outcroppings, and gentle wildflower meadows dictate the flow of play. Among the massive bunkers and hardwoods, you'll discover golf in its purest state — and an experience unlike any other. Tightly mowed zoysia fairways and manicured bentgrass greens create an incredible surface for play as the course winds along the river valley and into the landscape's secluded hills and draws. Native grasses, wicked bunkers, and exceptional long-range mountain views complete the experience of this award-winning course. Beyond the greens, enjoy the lively atmosphere of the Tavern, unwind in the comfort of our Hillside Cottages, and create lasting memories on our championship course. Discover the perfect blend of relaxation and recreation at Old Toccoa Farm Resort.
Call for a Tee Time: 706-946-GOLF

Blue Ridge Scenic Railway

Rides for Every Season

Your journey begins at the historic depot in downtown Blue Ridge, Georgia, where riders embark on either a two-hour or four-hour 26-mile roundtrip journey along the Toccoa River through the North Georgia countryside to the quaint sister towns of McCaysville, Georgia and Copperhill, Tennessee. Passengers who choose the express ride will then return back to Blue Ridge, while those who choose the extended ride enjoy a layover to enjoy all the little mountain towns have to offer before returning back to the depot!

Rules & Regulations

Blue Ridge RV Resort policies for a safe and enjoyable visit

General Guidelines & Declarations
Arrival / Departure Times
Check-in: 1:00 PM  |  Check-out: 11:00 AM. If you plan to stay longer, the earlier you re-register, the better your chance of keeping your same site. We encourage booking as early as possible — rates, availability, and site selection may be limited based on seasonality.
Prohibited
No tent/primitive camping. No charging of Electric Vehicles (EVs) on your campsite. No open carry of firearms. No parking on the grass or empty sites. No loud music from your site or vehicle. No drunken or disorderly conduct. No 4-wheelers, ATVs, or other off-road vehicles.
Noise Ordinance
Guests must comply with the Fannin County Noise Control Ordinance.
Payment & Cancellation Policies
Deposits
50% of the entire booked stay is due at reservation confirmation. Remaining balance is due at check-in. Weekly and monthly guests must pay in full prior to check-in.
Cancellations (Nightly Guests)
Less than 72 hours before arrival: entire amount paid is forfeited.
Less than 14 days before arrival (but before the 72-hour window): full credit issued for a future stay, valid 1 full year from issue date.
14+ days before arrival: full refund of deposit.

Register and prepay only for the time you are certain you are staying. A cancelled reservation that is not rescheduled incurs a $25 administration fee.
Extended Stay (1 Month+)
Cancelling 30+ days before arrival: full voucher issued, valid 1 full year from issue date — dates may be changed or used as a credit on a future reservation. Cancelling less than 2 weeks before arrival: no future credit or voucher granted.

Rates are subject to change at any time — any credit applied to a new reservation is responsible for the difference due to a rate change. Camping is an outdoor experience, so no refunds or vouchers are provided due to the discomfort of nature. No refunds or credits for early departures. Holidays require a 3-day minimum stay.
Early Departures
Refunds, vouchers, and credits will not be issued for early departures — no exceptions.
RV Resort Rules
Quiet Time
10:00 PM – 8:00 AM. Please be considerate of others — quiet time is strictly enforced by on-site security/management.
Speed Limit
5 MPH or less throughout the property.
Sewer
Your sewer hose must be fastened at both ends using a donut.
Children
Children under 16 should always be under parent/guardian supervision and never left unattended. You are solely responsible and liable for their behavior.
Visitors
Guests are welcome to have visitors and are responsible for their visitors' actions. All visitors must register at the RV Resort office upon arrival and exit the resort by 10:00 PM or additional fees will apply.
Campsite Cleanliness
Sites must be clean and uncluttered as determined by management. No excessive yard decorations, fencing, or personal items stored under/around your RV. No inoperative vehicles or repair work (including oil changes) on site. No refrigerators or freezers outside, no window AC units, no porches, permanent structures, hot tubs, or storage buildings. RVs must be clean and well maintained — no tarps, broken windows, or anything deemed unsightly by management. Pop-up canopies and pet fencing are allowed.

All RVs must be in workable condition and/or have an accompanying tow vehicle, per county ordinance. Inoperable, immobile, or broken-down RVs/vehicles will be towed and removed at the owner's expense.
🗑️ Trash
All trash must be bagged, tied, and left at the end of your campsite for pickup, 10 AM – 4 PM. Household bagged trash only — no appliances, mattresses, batteries, hazardous materials (including motor oil), or other large items. Placing trash outside the receptacle may result in fines.
Bicycles
For your safety, do not ride bicycles on walkways or in the RV Resort after dark. Always wear a helmet.
Golf Carts
Permitted but must be declared at check-in with an insurance binder. Driver must have a valid license; anyone under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Must obey all traffic regulations.
🏊 Swimming Pool
Heated, generally open April 1 – Thanksgiving Day. No lifeguard on duty. Anyone under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. No large inflatables. No pets or glass in the pool area per state law. Swim diapers required for ages 3 and under or anyone not potty trained.
Conduct & Ejection
Disorderly conduct, drunkenness, illegal drugs, obscene or vulgar behavior will not be tolerated. Any violation of these rules is grounds for dismissal from the property. No refunds are given for weather, or if you are asked to leave or choose to leave early.
🐾 Pets
Well-behaved, vaccinated pets are welcome, but must be on a leash when outside your vehicle or attended in the designated off-leash pet area. No retractable leashes. Always clean up after your pet. Do not leave pets tethered or penned outside when you are absent — owners are responsible for their pet(s) and their actions.
Restrooms & Laundry
Please leave the laundry room as you found it and don't leave laundry unattended — the resort is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged items. If machines need attention, notify onsite staff/management.
Vehicle Washing
Washing vehicles in the RV Resort is strictly prohibited. Ask office staff for a recommended local vehicle wash.
Insurance
All RVs should be equipped with a surge protector due to safety and insurance requirements.
Internet
Wi-Fi is offered free of charge with your reservation. Login credentials are provided at check-in; availability and security are not guaranteed — Wi-Fi is by nature a shared, unsecured connection.
Firewood
In compliance with the USDA & Georgia Forestry Commission, bringing firewood into the park from out of state is prohibited due to the risk of transporting destructive foreign pests.
Fire Safety
No fireworks are allowed. No open flame devices besides grills in public areas, and no toxic chemicals or other hazardous equipment. Fires are permitted unless prohibited by local ordinance during fire danger — fully extinguish any fire before bed or leaving the area.
Parking
Maximum of 1 RV and 2 vehicles per campsite. Boat/trailer parking is available only upon request due to limited space.
Alcohol
Permitted for guests 21+ on your site only. You are liable for your own safety, the safety of others, and any damage caused by you or your party. Do not drive (including golf carts or bicycles) after drinking. No alcohol in the pool or clubhouse. No drunkenness tolerated.
Respecting Other Campsites
Please do not cross over or encroach upon occupied campsites. After packing up, check for trash and move back anything relocated — report anything broken to staff.
Smoking
No smoking in the clubhouse, laundry facility, or restrooms. Dispose of cigarette butts properly — designated smoking areas are strictly enforced.
🐾 Pet Policy Agreement
By bringing a pet, you agree: not to take your pet(s) into any areas other than those marked "pet friendly"; not to leave your pet(s) outside unattended; to pick up after your pet(s) and properly dispose of waste; to keep your pet(s) leashed at all times, including within your campsite; and to keep your pet(s) from being a nuisance or hazard to others. You declare your pet(s) has never shown a vicious nature or bitten anyone, and you register your pet(s) as under your care, taking full responsibility for their actions.
Liability & Compliance
The Fine Print
Blue Ridge RV Resort assumes no responsibility for injury, accident, damage, or loss of individual property from any cause of nature. Management is not responsible for loss due to fire, theft, accidents, or any other cause. Management reserves the right to refuse service to anyone and to evict without prior notice anyone who creates a disturbance, nuisance, or deliberately breaks the resort's policies — eviction is immediate and without reimbursement of camping fees.
Terms & Conditions
By accepting a reservation, Guest waives, releases, and discharges all claims against Blue Ridge RV Resort for personal injury, accidents, or property damage arising from use of the property, and agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold the resort harmless from related losses or claims. Guest is responsible and liable for the actions and safety of everyone in their group.

Blue Ridge RV Resort is privately owned. Pet owners assume full responsibility for injuries or damage caused by their pet. Property and buildings are monitored — Guest grants the resort the right to take and use photographs of Guest and their property for lawful purposes including publicity, illustration, advertising, and web content. Background checks are performed on each 18+ member of the party prior to Extended Stay Agreement execution.

RV Resorts are full of fun and excitement for all — but they can also be dangerous. Camp at your own risk.
