Swimming Pools & Hot Tub

Talona Ridge RV Resort

The opportunities to swim at Grandview's Quiet Pool, the Hot Tub at Grandview Pool, and the Family Pool at Owl Landing on Falcon Ridge are the most popular amenities at the Resort. Heated and open year-round (freezing temperatures aside), it makes for a highlight during your stay.
Eagle Crest
The Quiet Pool at Grandview
The Resort's most commanding views are visible from both the pool and hot tub at Grandview as well as a lower viewing deck, facing the mountains. The pool area is conveniently located between Grandview Hall and the Fitness Center/Pool House. All sites from every level of the Resort have access to use the pool.
Falcon Ridge

Tucked in a more sheltered corner of the resort and just steps away from Talons Grille, the Lawn at Owl Landing, and other relaxing spaces, the Family Pool is the perfect spot to swim, splash, and unwind with the whole crew. Designed with families in mind, it's a welcoming retreat where fun and connection comes naturally.

9:00 AM – 10:00 PM
Owl Landing Pool has been intentionally placed for families to enjoy. Grandview Pool will be for adults only.

Golf Club

Two Championship 18-Hole Courses

Overview
Located minutes from downtown Atlanta, Stone Mountain Golf Club features two championship courses with scenic views of historic Stone Mountain and Stone Mountain Lake.
Stonemont Course
Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. in 1969. A par 70 layout stretching 6,863 yards with narrow fairways, strategically placed bunkers and numerous stands of Georgia pines.

Has hosted numerous national professional events and Georgia amateur championships.
Par 70 6,863 Yards Bent Grass Greens
Lakemont Course
Designed by Georgia native John LaFoy. The front 9 wind around Stone Mountain Lake with 6 holes having water in play. The back 9 features rock outcroppings with 4 water holes and dramatic elevation changes.
Lake Views 10 Water Holes Elevation Changes

Terms & Conditions

Please read carefully before your stay

Please read our terms and conditions carefully. We desire that every guest enjoys their stay. Adhering to the terms and conditions will enhance that experience. These terms & conditions are subject to change at any time and for any reason at the discretion of management.
RV Policy
• All recreational vehicles must be currently registered and maintained in good condition and in running order.

• Talona Ridge RV Resort must approve any RV 10 years old or older prior to making reservations. Email info@talonaridge.com for approval. Reservations should not be made prior to receiving an approval from management.

• If an RV is NOT approved and a reservation has already been made, it will fall under the cancellation policy. Any reservations made prior to an approval and cancelled the same day or within 14 days of the reservation will also fall under the cancellation policy.

• No RV deemed to be in "poor" condition (e.g., broken or cracked windows, flat tires, rusted or badly dented or in need of paint or extremely unkept or excessively dirty) will be allowed in the RV Resort.

• Approved recreational vehicles include Class A, Class B and Class C Motor coaches, fifth wheels, hard sided travel trailers, and Toy Haulers.

• Cargo Trailers and Living Quarter Trailers are NOT permitted.

• Truck-campers, pop-up canvas trailers, trailers with canvas extensions — often referred to as hybrids — are NOT permitted.

• No tent camping is allowed anywhere on site.

• All RV's must be leveled and supported with the proper equipment when located on an RV site.
The goal of Talona Ridge RV Resort is to provide each guest with a relaxing and fun, but quiet, family-oriented experience. To ensure all guests' safe enjoyment of our facilities, we ask that you read and adhere to the following:
Hours & Check-In / Check-Out
Welcome Center Office Hours
9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Every Day. Welcome Center hours are subject to change.
Arrival Check-In Time
1:00 PM – 4:00 PM Every Day (After-hours check-in until 9:00 PM). Please call 706-636-2267 if you will be arriving later than 6:00 PM. Latest arrival allowed is 9:00 PM. For after-hours check-ins, please call 762-282-7770 at least 15 minutes prior to arrival, and one of our staff will meet you at the Welcome Center.
Check-Out Time
Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Late check-outs will incur a $25 charge per hour, and are not permitted unless pre-approved by management, depending on availability.
Deposits & Refunds
Deposits
Reservations of 3 nights or less require payment in full at the time the reservation is made. Reservations of 4 nights or longer require a deposit of half the total reservation fee, with the remaining balance due 14 days prior to arrival.
Refunds
RVing is an outdoor experience, therefore no refunds or credits will be provided due to the discomfort of nature, including windstorms, hail, heavy rain, or icy conditions. No refunds or credits will be made for early departures or evictions.

Talona Ridge will not offer refunds due to poor internet connection, interrupted cell service, TVs, or other electronic connection issues.

Talona Ridge RV Resort exclusively determines refund requests. Requests for special refunds or credits must be made in writing to management, providing full proof of emergency circumstances claimed. Any refund processed will be refunded in the same form it was originally paid.
🌙 Quiet Hours
10:00 PM – 8:00 AM. Please be considerate of others. No loud music or TV noise at any time. Use of generators is prohibited.
Changes to Booking
If you would like to add a day(+) to your booking, request to be moved to another section of the resort, or adjust your existing booking in any way, please call our Welcome Center.

If you wish to move your booking dates and are within 14 days of your arrival date, it is considered a re-booking and will be subject to a $25 fee. Once a guest arrives on site, cancellations or changes cannot be made in order to receive a partial or full refund.
No-Show Policy
Reserved sites will be held for your occupancy until 11:00 AM the day after you are scheduled to arrive. If you have not arrived by that time or communicated with the resort about your arrival plans, the guest will be responsible for the full amount of the stay with no refund given, and your site will become available for booking by another guest.
Damaged & Missing Items
Any damages or missing items by you or your guests in your accommodations as a result of your stay will be assessed and billed accordingly to your credit card.
Occupancy & Speed Limit
Occupancy
The maximum number of people allowed per site is six (6), including children.
Speed Limit
For the safety of all our guests, we ask that you maintain a speed limit of 7 MPH throughout the entire resort, for ALL motorized vehicles. The speed limit will be strictly enforced. Offenders may be asked to leave.
Smoking / Vaping
Smoking and vaping are only permitted at your own pad site. Please refrain from smoking in any other areas within the resort.
📦 Packages & Deliveries
• We welcome packages during your stay, but ask that you do not run a heavy shipping/delivery business while here.

• Food Delivery — we allow meals and/or groceries to be delivered, but you must let the Welcome Center know you're expecting a delivery and keep your phone available so front desk can call you (with guest name and site number) when it arrives. Without this, the driver will not be let through the gate.

• After-hours food deliveries will not be able to come past the front gate — please meet your delivery near the Welcome Center.
Bath Suites
• Smoking, vaping, drinking, eating, and bathing of pets in the bath suites is prohibited.

• Please clean up after yourself and check for personal items before leaving; management is not responsible for lost items.

• Please make sure all water sources are completely turned off before leaving. Report any running or dripping faucets you can't turn off.

• Children under the age of 13 must be accompanied by an adult.
Children
• Parents/Guardians are responsible for the appropriate behavior of their children/grandchildren at all times. Parent/Guardian will be held financially liable for damages caused by their children/grandchildren.

• Children MUST be supervised in bath suites, restrooms, the pool area, pavilions, pickleball courts, and Grandview Hall.

• Only licensed drivers with a valid ID may operate golf carts.

• Children on bikes, skateboards, skates, scooters, etc. may only ride on black-top surfaces (not grass or other pad sites); may not ride around buildings on Eagle Crest or the pickleball courts; may not ride around or in the pool area or fitness center (wheels must be left outside those gates); and may not ride around any part of the resort after dark.
🏊 Pool
Pool Hours
8:00 AM – 10:00 PM Spring & Summer  |  8:00 AM – 9:00 PM Fall & Winter
• No lifeguard on duty; swim at your own risk.

• Please rinse or shower before entering pool. Showers provided within pool area.

• Children under 16, and those who cannot swim, must always be accompanied by an adult.

• Infants and toddlers must always wear waterproof swimming trunks/swim diapers in the pool area.

• No rough play, including running, pushing, shoving, yelling, or screaming.

• No diving in any part of the pool.

• No smoking/vaping in pool area. No pets are allowed in the pool area.

• Please clean up after yourself; management is not responsible for lost or stolen items.

• No glass containers in or around pool.

• No more than 62 guests in the pool at one time.

• Appropriate swim attire is required. Cut-off jeans are prohibited in pool and hot tub areas.
Hot Tub — Safety Warnings
• Please rinse or shower before entering. Appropriate swim attire required. No jumping or diving. Please do not use soaps or oils in the hot tub.

• Risk of fetus damage: pregnant women and small children should not use a hot tub prior to medical consultation.

• Risk of drowning: persons with heart disease, diabetes, high/low blood pressure, or other health problems should not enter without medical consultation. Do not use while under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Use caution when bathing alone; overexposure may cause nausea, dizziness, and fainting. Do not use if the suction fitting is missing, broken, or loose.

• Children under 16 are prohibited from using the hot tub.

• Risk of injury: check temperature before each use (should not exceed 104°F). Enter and exit slowly. Keep glass and breakable objects out of the area.

• Risk of shock: never place electrical appliances within 5 feet of the hot tub. Do not operate during severe weather (electrical storms, tornadoes, etc.).
Grandview Event Hall
Hours
9:00 AM – 9:00 PM Every Day
• No smoking or vaping permitted in or around the Event Hall or terrace.

• The pool area is separate from the Event Hall and may not be reserved.

• No running inside the Event Hall.

• Children under 13 must be accompanied and supervised by an adult.

• No pets allowed inside, except registered service animals.

• Please keep watch over personal belongings; management is not responsible for lost or stolen items.

• No wet swimwear allowed inside at any time.

• No outside alcohol is allowed when the bar is open.
🔥 Lighting & Fire Use
Wood Burning Fire Pits
Please only burn wood purchased from our general store at the Welcome Center (free delivery to your site if ordered before 3:00 PM) — this helps stop the spread of tree-killing insects and disease hidden in outside firewood. All campfires must be kept in designated areas such as the fire pit at your site. Never leave a fire unattended and always fully extinguish before leaving. Please do not move the fire pits or use them for warming/cooking food. No portable deep fryers or portable grills in common areas, pool areas, picnic tables, or pavilions.
Gas Fire Pits
No food cooking on gas fire pits. Marshmallow roasting is only acceptable on wood or coal fires at your site.
Lighting
No fireworks allowed. Overnight use of overhead outside lighting, spotlights, high directional beam lighting, or awning lighting is prohibited.
Fitness Center
• For resort guests only; visitors must be accompanied by a guest. Use equipment at your own risk — management assumes no responsibility for bodily injury.

• Closed-toe athletic shoes and appropriate gym attire required. No sandals or flip flops.

• Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult; children under 12 may not use the fitness center.

• Please return weights to the rack after use — do not drop weights. Please wipe down equipment after use with provided disinfectant wipes.

• No food or drinks except bottled water. No pets except assistance animals. No smoking or vaping.

• Persons under the influence of alcohol or drugs are prohibited.

• Please limit use of treadmills, elliptical, and recumbent bike to 30 minutes while others are waiting.

• Notify management of any injuries and report malfunctioning equipment immediately. Call 911 in case of emergency.
Pickleball Courts
Hours
8:00 AM till dusk, daily
• Use at your own risk. Courts are to be used for pickleball only.

• No food or drinks except water. No pets. Please do not hang on the nets.

• No skateboarding/roller skating/blading or bicycles/scooters/hoverboards on courts.

• Children under 13 must be accompanied by an adult.

• Tennis shoes or soft-soled shoes only — no cleats.

• Players waiting to play must wait outside the courts. Equipment is available for loan at the Welcome Center.
Pavilions & Grills
• No smoking or vaping at the pavilions.

• Please turn off grill/fire pit when not in use and never leave an active grill unattended. Please scrape the grill when finished (tools provided) and make sure gas is completely shut off after use.

• Please dispose of trash properly and return tables/equipment to the condition they should be for the next guest.

• Please do not place portable grills on picnic tables. No portable deep fryers. No portable grills in common areas, pool areas, picnic tables, or pavilions — use your own site or the grills provided at the pavilions.
🚩 Flag Policy
American, Canadian, Military (Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Coast Guard), POW, MIA, and veterans, sports, and decorative flags (holiday, seasonal, or garden) are allowed. Political or other promotional flags/signs are not permitted.
🐾 Pets & Pet Owners
• Maximum of two (2) common household pets (dogs and cats) per site without prior authorization. With pre-approval, additional pets are $5/day each. No livestock, poultry, reptiles, or insects.

• No dangerous, aggressive, or poisonous/venomous pets of any kind. Management may decline or require removal of any pet at any time, for any reason.

• All pets must be registered with office staff within 24 hours of check-in; vaccination records must be available upon request.

• All pets must wear a collar identifying owner and phone number, and be on a non-retractable leash (max 6 ft) at all times, except while enjoying the "Bear's Den" dog park.

• No pet may be left unattended outside the RV or left inside continually barking. Owners must immediately pick up all animal excrement — failure to do so results in a $10 charge per occurrence.

• Pets are NOT permitted in public buildings/amenity areas, including the Welcome Center, Grandview Hall, swimming pool, Fitness Center, and bath suites.

• Service/assistance/comfort animals must be registered; ADA-defined service animals are not charged a pet fee, but must comply with all aspects of this policy.

• The indoor Dog Wash Station is located in Hawk Valley near the dog park.
👕 Laundry Rooms
• Use at your own risk; management is not responsible for damaged or lost items.

• Do not color, tint, or dye clothing/materials in machines. Do not sit on counters or machines. Do not overload washers or dryers.

• Please discard dryer lint after each use and remove clothes promptly after each cycle. Check machines for foreign objects before use.

• No smoking or vaping in laundry rooms.
🚗 Golf Cart Rules
• Maintain a speed limit of 7 MPH throughout the resort. Resort rental golf carts are for use inside the resort only — may not be driven offsite.

• No standing while cart is in motion. No one may sit in the driver's lap — everyone must have their own seat, max 5 people per cart.

• Keep hands and feet inside the cart while in motion. Stay on resort roads or golf cart paths. Only those with a valid driver's license may operate a golf cart. No texting or cell phone use while driving.

• Golf carts do NOT have right-of-way — always yield to other motor vehicles and pedestrians.

• Rental carts can be picked up at 1:00 PM the day reserved, and must be returned by 11:00 AM the day due, with belongings removed and trash disposed of.
Non-Residence
Talona Ridge RV Resort is a private lodging establishment intended for temporary stays by transient guests and is not to be used as a residence. Permanent living quarters, structures, or vehicles used for permanent living quarters are prohibited. No wood, metal, or poly storage sheds.
Pedestals
Please make sure all hookups are completely disconnected from the pedestal at your site before pulling off. Damage to pedestals will be incurred by guests at time of inspection — pedestal replacement costs $1,500.00.
Respectfulness
• Please do not walk or drive through other guests' sites; keep your possessions on your own site only.

• No inflatable pools, playhouses, pens, or tents of any kind may be set up at your site.

• Please be respectful of neighbors and keep noise at an acceptable level, even during the day.

• Please use dryers provided at the laundry facilities — clotheslines and hanging clothes outside is prohibited. Rugs and blankets are prohibited on the grass.

• Alcohol consumption permitted by legal age adults only; minors are prohibited from consuming any alcoholic drinks.

• Management reserves the right to eject any guest engaging in excessive alcohol consumption, physical violence, obscene or unruly behavior, profanity, possession of illegal drugs, or display of firearms — immediately, without refund.
RV Washing & Repair
Vehicles and RVs may not be washed or repaired on site without advanced approval of management and proof of the repair vendor's liability insurance. Guests may purchase a wash permit at the Welcome Center for $25, allowing washing on-site between 9 AM and 5 PM.
🗑️ Trash Disposal
Please make sure trash bags are sealed properly and placed at the end of your pad no later than 10:00 AM. Please do not leave trash out overnight.
Vehicles
• One vehicle is included in the site fee. One extra vehicle may be parked on your site; vehicles that cannot be parked on-site incur an additional $5/day charge. Do not park on the grass. All vehicles must have a valid state license plate, parking pass, and proof of insurance.

• All ATV/UTVs and other large terrain vehicles are prohibited. Only car, truck, or adequately insured golf cart permitted. Only licensed drivers with a valid ID may operate golf carts.

• Trailers are permitted in management-designated areas: $5/day for trailers up to 20 ft, $10/day for trailers over 20 ft. Management is not responsible for theft or damage to personal items/equipment on resort grounds.
Visitors of Resort Guests
• All guests are responsible for their visitors. Each invited visitor must register at the Welcome Center. Visitor fee is $5 per person, per day, charged to the residing guest's site, and must be announced to management in advance.

• Guests/visitors may not park on empty sites or grass areas — designated areas only.

• You are responsible for your visitors and any damage they may cause. Visitors may not bring pets. All visiting guests must leave by 10:00 PM. Visitors may not use showers or laundry at the resort.
💧 Water & Sewer Hookup
Water
City water pressure at Talona Ridge is between 50–65 PSI. It is recommended that most RVs not exceed 60 PSI — please consider using a water pressure regulator. Your RV water connection must be watertight; contact management if you notice leaking.
Sewer (the WHITE cap)
All sewer hookups must have sewer rings or elbows to prevent backup or spills — a $300.00 charge will be issued for sewer spills. There are two caps at your site: use the WHITE cap for sewer. The blue cap is for water shut-off.
🦌 Wildlife
Help us keep wildlife wild by not feeding the animals. Please do not leave food out overnight and keep trash in provided receptacles.
Code of Conduct
RV owners, family members, guests, or visitors who create disturbance or behave in a manner that constitutes a threat or substantial nuisance to other guests will not be allowed to remain on the property. This decision is solely up to Talona Ridge RV Resort management.

Park Information
🌙 Quiet Hours
11 PM to 7 AM. For noise complaints call the SMP Police Non-Emergency Number 770-498-5675.
🗑️ Garbage Pickup
Curbside at your site between 9 AM – 4 PM. There are no dumpsters — trash will be picked up curbside only.
👕 Laundry Facilities
Located at Comfort Stations #3, #7 and #9. Use the Aaxon Pay App. A QR Code is in the Laundry Room.
🔍 Lost & Found
Located at the Welcome Center. Technology items (phones, computers, wallets, credit cards) will be turned over to SMP Public Safety.
📦 Mail Drop & Package Delivery
At the Welcome Center. Please include your Name and Site number when having something delivered.
🏊 Swimming Pool Passes
Available in the Welcome Center. You must present one for yourself or family when asked by a staff member.
🚗 UBER / LYFT
Available for guests. Drivers will be charged a $5.00 fee at the entrance gate — please advise your driver in advance.
⚡ Weather Alert System
If lightning is detected within 10 miles of the park a horn will sound once for 15 seconds. The horn sounds 3 times rapidly when lightning has cleared the area. If severe weather is approaching, the system will sound advising you to seek shelter.
