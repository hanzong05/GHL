Fishing

Stone Mountain Lake

Hours & Access
Fishing is permitted in Stone Mountain Lake from 6:00 AM until midnight. Access to the park is included with a daily or annual parking pass. A valid Georgia Fishing License is required.
Fish in the Lake
🐟 Bass
🐠 Carp
🐡 Crappie
🐟 Catfish
🐡 Bream
Boating Rules
• Private boats allowed daily during daylight hours
• Launch from the Public Boat Ramp near Evergreen Pavilion
• Outboard motors 10 hp max
• No alcoholic beverages on privately-owned boats

Golf Club

Two Championship 18-Hole Courses

Overview
Located minutes from downtown Atlanta, Stone Mountain Golf Club features two championship courses with scenic views of historic Stone Mountain and Stone Mountain Lake.
Stonemont Course
Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. in 1969. A par 70 layout stretching 6,863 yards with narrow fairways, strategically placed bunkers and numerous stands of Georgia pines.

Has hosted numerous national professional events and Georgia amateur championships.
Par 70 6,863 Yards Bent Grass Greens
Lakemont Course
Designed by Georgia native John LaFoy. The front 9 wind around Stone Mountain Lake with 6 holes having water in play. The back 9 features rock outcroppings with 4 water holes and dramatic elevation changes.
Lake Views 10 Water Holes Elevation Changes

Rules & Regulations

Park policies for a safe and enjoyable visit

🚨 Emergency Numbers
SMP Police Emergency
770-413-5333
Non-Emergency
770-498-5675
Check-In & Check-Out
Check-In Time
2:00 PM — A valid Driver's License or government ID is required. Guests must be 18 or older to register.
Check-Out Time
Lodging: 11:00 AM  |  RVs & Tents: 12:00 PM
Site Changes & Extensions
Register by 10:00 AM to move to a new site or extend your stay. For late check-out, call the office on your departure day.
Maximum Length of Stay
RV Sites: 14 days (see manager to extend)  |  Lodging & Tent Sites: 7 days
Camp Credit
May be available for RV/Tent Sites if you leave by 12 PM at least 1 day before your scheduled departure. No Camp Credit during holidays.
Site Rules
Occupancy
Each RV Site is for one RV only. Pricing is based on 5 guests per site. Additional guests are $5.00/night. No refund on Lodging once checked in.
Water Pressure
Water Pressure Regulators must be used at all sites.
Tents
Tents are only allowed on designated tent sites (brown and yellow sites). One tent on Primitive Sites, two on Yellow Sites.
Vehicles on Site
Two vehicles max per site (car, truck, van, SUV, street-legal motorcycle) plus a golf cart. ATVs, UTVs, Go Carts, and Dirt Bikes are prohibited. All vehicles must have a current tag and hang tag — get your hang tag at the Welcome Center with your receipt.
Vehicle Camping
Camping in your vehicle, unless self-contained, is prohibited per Stone Mountain Park Ordinances.
Quiet Hours & Conduct
🌙 Quiet Hours
11:00 PM – 7:00 AM. Call SMP PD Non-Emergency for noise complaints: 770-498-5675
Speed Limit
15 MPH inside the campground.
Children & Bicycles
Parents are responsible for the actions and safety of their children. Georgia law requires all riders age 15 and younger to wear a helmet when biking.
Pool
Registered campground guests may use the pool (open seasonally). No lifeguard on duty — swim at your own risk.
Fireworks
Fireworks of any kind are prohibited by law. Defacing, cutting down, or driving nails into trees is also prohibited.
Waste & Environment
🗑️ Garbage Pickup
Curbside at your site 9 AM – 4 PM. Bag all trash in kitchen-size bags or larger.
Wastewater
Georgia State Health Department prohibits emptying any liquids (including dishwater) at campsites. Use the designated Dump Station for all wastewater. Do not pour fats, oils, or grease into sinks, dump stations, or sewage lines.
🐾 Pets
Leash & Control
Keep pets on a leash and under control at all times. Clean up after your pet in all areas.
Unattended Pets
Do not leave dogs unattended outside. Keep pets quiet during quiet hours.
Lodging & Welcome Center
Pets are NOT allowed in or around lodging areas or in the Campground Welcome Center.
Aggressive Dogs
Pets that interfere with the guest experience or show aggression will be required to leave the campground.
⚡ Weather Alert System
Horn Signals
1 long blast (15 sec) — Lightning detected within 10 miles.
3 short blasts — Lightning has cleared.
Continuous horn — Severe weather approaching, seek shelter immediately.
Emergency Shelters
🏠 CS#4 — Trail F  |  CS#5 — Trail C
🏠 CS#6 — Trail A  |  CS#8 — Trail N
Liability & Compliance
Waiver Required
All campground guests must sign a waiver at or before check-in. Stone Mountain Park is not responsible for lost/stolen articles or personal injury/damage from any cause.
Right to Remove
Stone Mountain Park Campground reserves the right to remove any guest from the premises, without refund, for failure to comply with campground rules, park regulations, or applicable laws.

Park Information
🌙 Quiet Hours
11 PM to 7 AM. For noise complaints call the SMP Police Non-Emergency Number 770-498-5675.
🗑️ Garbage Pickup
Curbside at your site between 9 AM – 4 PM. There are no dumpsters — trash will be picked up curbside only.
👕 Laundry Facilities
Located at Comfort Stations #3, #7 and #9. Use the Aaxon Pay App. A QR Code is in the Laundry Room.
🔍 Lost & Found
Located at the Welcome Center. Technology items (phones, computers, wallets, credit cards) will be turned over to SMP Public Safety.
📦 Mail Drop & Package Delivery
At the Welcome Center. Please include your Name and Site number when having something delivered.
🏊 Swimming Pool Passes
Available in the Welcome Center. You must present one for yourself or family when asked by a staff member.
🚗 UBER / LYFT
Available for guests. Drivers will be charged a $5.00 fee at the entrance gate — please advise your driver in advance.
⚡ Weather Alert System
If lightning is detected within 10 miles of the park a horn will sound once for 15 seconds. The horn sounds 3 times rapidly when lightning has cleared the area. If severe weather is approaching, the system will sound advising you to seek shelter.
